
# LLM_REASONING_SURVEY Paper List

## Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning

This section includes research papers focusing on Chain-of-Thought reasoning. Chain-of-Thought reasoning is essential for improving the logical flow and performance of large language models in tasks requiring complex thought processes.

### Papers:

1. **[Chain-of-Table: Evolving Tables in the Reasoning Chain for Table Understanding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04398)**  
2. **[Chain-of-Thought Reasoning Without Prompting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04398)**
3. **Chain-of-Knowledge: Integrating Knowledge Reasoning into Large Language Models by Learning from Knowledge Graphs**
4. **Self-Harmonized Chain of Thought**
5. **To CoT or not to CoT? Chain-of-thought helps mainly with math and symbolic reasoning**
